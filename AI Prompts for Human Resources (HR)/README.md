## How to Use This Collection

The main guide establishes the framework and the recruitment-first prompts; the companion files extend across the employee lifecycle. Three rules govern everything:

1. **People data is the most sensitive data class** — anonymize before any AI use; patterns and roles, not names and details; real records in approved HR systems only
2. **Decisions about people stay with accountable humans** — AI structures evidence, drafts communications, and prepares conversations; evaluation, ratings, discipline, and employment decisions never transfer to a tool
3. **Legal and formal processes lead where exposure exists** — discrimination, harassment, termination, and pay matters follow counsel's guidance and your organization's formal process from the start

For the underlying prompt techniques (structured outputs for screening, chaining for onboarding pipelines, evaluation of HR prompts), see the [Advanced Prompt Engineering Techniques](../Advanced%20Prompt%20Engineering%20Techniques/) section.
